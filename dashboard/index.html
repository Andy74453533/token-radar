Every figure comes from one sealed snapshot, and every value is fitted from it: a power law through the protocols whose token already trades, backtested before it ranks anything. The board ranks only protocols whose record shows no token; protocols whose token already trades are shown apart, with the market's own value, and never mixed into the ranking.

Click a column heading to sort. Click a row for its detail view: the fitted breakdown, the live protocols it is priced against, and its farm plan.

Reading the board. “Records no token” means the snapshot's own record carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol — it is not proof a protocol has no token. Implied value is this entry's own claim from the snapshot's figures, fitted and backtested, never a prediction that a token is issued, or when.
